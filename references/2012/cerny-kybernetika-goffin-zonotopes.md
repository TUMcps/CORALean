# Goffin's algorithm for zonotopes

M. Černý. "Goffin's algorithm for zonotopes". Kybernetika, 48(5):890-906, 2012.

**Source:** https://dml.cz/bitstream/handle/10338.dmlcz/143088/Kybernetika_48-2012-5_5.pdf (the Czech Digital Mathematics Library copy of the journal paper, 17 pages, read in full; text extracted with `pypdf`)  
**Inventory:** confirmed (the numbered items are Theorem and Definition 1.1, Definitions 1.2-1.3, Theorem 1.4, Lemmas 3.1-3.2 and 3.4, Theorem 3.3, Corollary 3.5, Lemmas 4.1-4.2, Lemma and Definition 4.3, Lemma 4.4 and equations (1)-(19); the unnumbered statements that make a claim are rows named by section)

The paper behind the initial ellipse and the inscribed-ball test of CORA's `zonotope/ellipsoid` (`priv_encEllipsoid`, `priv_inscEllipsoid`), via [Gaßmann and Althoff 2020](../2020/gassmann-acc-zonotope-ellipsoid.md): Goffin's ellipsoid method with parallel cuts adapted to zonotopes given by generators, whose membership oracle is a linear program. The initial ellipse of `Sec. 4.1` and the axis test of `Eq. 17`-`Eq. 18` are what CORA uses; the algorithm of `Sec. 4.6` is not. Formalized here: the zonotope as the evolution of a point under its generators and its normal forms (`Def. 1.3`, `Lemma 3.1`, `Lemma 3.2`), the degree of a point, the initial ellipses, the parallel-cut ellipse of `Lemma 4.3` containing the cut ball and shrinking the volume by `κ_ε` (`Lemma 4.4`), the ball test, the separator of `Sec. 4.5` given a facet and its basis, and the algorithm of `Sec. 4.6` with the test and the separator as an oracle, and the face structure behind `Lemma 3.4` and `Cor. 3.5`: every exposed face is the zonotope of the generators its direction annihilates, and every proper face of a full-dimensional zonotope lies in a facet. The Löwner-John theorem, the minimality of the cut ellipse and the shallow-cut volume bound are restated from the literature [2, 7, 9, 11]. Everything stated in the bit size of the input is outside the count (`not-covered`), and so are the running times.

## Findings

**`Sec. 1.1 (ellipse)` — the image description omits the center.** The ellipse is defined as `E(E, s) := {x | (x - s)ᵀE⁻¹(x - s) ≤ 1} = {E^{1/2}(x - s) | ‖x‖ ≤ 1}`. The second set is the unit ball under `x ↦ E^{1/2}(x - s)`, which is centered at `-E^{1/2}s`; the first set is `{s + E^{1/2}x | ‖x‖ ≤ 1}`. For `n = 1`, `E = 1`, `s = 2` the first is `[1, 3]` and the second `[-3, -1]` (`printed_ellipse_image_counterexample`). The corrected equality is `mem_ofShape_iff`, which is what the later uses need (the unit ball is "the image of `E(E, 0)` under `ξ ↦ E^{-1/2}ξ`", with `s = 0`). [`MemOfShapeIff.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/Ellipsoid/Real/Theorems/MemOfShapeIff.lean), [`PrintedEllipseImage.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/Ellipsoid/Real/Counterexamples/PrintedEllipseImage.lean)

**`Eq. 16`, `Lemma 4.3`, `Lemma 4.4`, `Eq. 19`, `Sec. 4.6 (algorithm)` — the dimension.** The shape matrix of the parallel cut divides by `n - 1` and by `1 - γ²`; for `n = 1` the cut `|x| ≤ γ` of the interval `[-1, 1]` is already its own smallest ellipse. The lemmas say "the `n`-dimensional unit ball" without `n ≥ 2`; the formalization takes `2 ≤ n`, and the cut parameter `γ < 1/√n` as `nγ² < 1` (so that the matrix is positive definite, with eigenvalue `nγ²` along `c`). In the proof of `Lemma 4.4`, `γ = 1/√(n(1+ε))` is typeset ambiguously; it is the value for which `nγ² = 1/(1+ε)`, as the displayed matrix and the radius `1/(n√(1+ε))` of the ball of `Eq. 18` need. The remaining algebra of the proof is correct: the ratio of volumes is `√det E = (1+ε)^{-1/2}(1 + ε/((1+ε)(n-1)))^{(n-1)/2}`, bounded by `κ_ε = exp(1/√(1+ε) + ε/(2(1+ε)) - 1)`, which is below `1` since the exponent is `-(u - 1)²/(2u²)` for `u = √(1+ε) > 1`. [`CutVolumeLe.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/Ellipsoid/Real/Theorems/CutVolumeLe.lean)

The algorithm of `Sec. 4.6` cuts with `Lemma 4.3` in every iteration that does not stop, so it
inherits `n ≥ 2`; `GoffinSpec` takes it, and otherwise holds as the paper argues: the separator gives
`aᵀx ≤ b < aᵀx₀ ≤ ‖a‖γ` on `Z′`, central symmetry the other side of the slab, and `ξ ↦ E_j^{1/2}ξ`
carries the cut ellipse to `E(E_j^{1/2}EE_j^{1/2}, 0)`. [`GoffinSpec.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/Ellipsoid/Real/Theorems/GoffinSpec.lean)

**`Cor. 3.5` — iterating Lemma 3.4 does not keep `x` in the facet.** The proof is one sentence, "iterating Lemma 3.4 for at most `n - 1` times". Lemma 3.4 as stated gives, for each step, a facet through `xᵢ` and `xᵢ₊₁`; it says nothing of `x = x₀`, and sharing a facet is not transitive, so the facet the last step lands in need not contain `x`. Nor does it cover `deg(x) = n - 1`, where no step is taken: that a point of degree `n - 1` is interior to its facet is the converse of the remark before Thm. 3.3, which [1] does not state. Both are repaired by what Lemma 3.4's proof does and its statement omits — `x* = x + γ*g/2` is the midpoint of a segment of `Z` through `x`, so every face through `x*` contains `x` — and by the face structure; the corollary holds as printed. The formalization goes directly: the least face through `x` has dimension `≤ n - 1 < n`, so it is proper and lies in a facet `F = Z.face u` (`exists_facet_superset`), and `x*` is that zonotope's center, interior to it relative to its affine hull (`center_mem_intrinsicInterior`). [`ExistsFacetMemIntrinsicInterior.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/Zonotope/Real/Theorems/ExistsFacetMemIntrinsicInterior.lean)

**Minor.** `Sec. 4.5` places `x₀ = eᵢ₀/√(n(1+ε))` "on the boundary of the ball
`B′ := E(1/√(n(1+ε)) · I, 0)`"; with `E(E, s)` of shape `E`, that ball has radius
`(n(1+ε))^{-1/4}`, and the ball through `x₀` is `E(I/(n(1+ε)), 0)`. Nothing depends on it: the
separator needs `x₀ ∉ Z` alone.

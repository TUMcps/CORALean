# Constrained zonotopes: A new tool for set-based estimation and fault detection

J. K. Scott, D. M. Raimondo, G. R. Marseglia and R. D. Braatz. "Constrained zonotopes: A new tool for set-based estimation and fault detection". Automatica, 69:126-136, 2016.

**Source:** https://web.mit.edu/braatzgroup/Scott_Automatica_2016.pdf (the authors' group copy of the published article, 11 pages, read in full; text extracted with `pypdf`)  
**Inventory:** confirmed (the numbered items are Definitions 1-3, Propositions 1-5, Theorem 1, Remarks 1-2 and the appendix's Algorithm 1; each of Definition, Proposition and Remark has its own counter)

Where `ConZonotope` comes from: a zonotope whose coefficients also satisfy `Aξ = b`, closed
under linear maps, Minkowski sums and intersection, and equal to the class of polytopes. The
formalization keeps the paper's exact closure identities, the interval-refinement
algorithm, emptiness and membership by one linear program, the lifted zonotope and the
enclosure statements of its complexity reduction (rescaling, constraint elimination,
lift-then-reduce), and `Thm. 1` in both directions; the reduction heuristics and the
estimation and fault-detection applications are not formalized.
[Raghuraman and Koeln](../2022/raghuraman-koeln-constrained-zonotopes.md) restate the
appendix algorithm as their Alg. 1 and the elimination as their Thm. 2.

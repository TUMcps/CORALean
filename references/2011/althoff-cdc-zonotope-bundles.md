# Zonotope Bundles for the Efficient Computation of Reachable Sets

M. Althoff and B. H. Krogh. "Zonotope Bundles for the Efficient Computation of Reachable Sets". In Proc. of the 50th IEEE Conference on Decision and Control and European Control Conference (CDC-ECC), pp. 6814-6821, 2011.

**Source:** https://skoge.folk.ntnu.no/prost/proceedings/cdc-ecc-2011/data/papers/1225.pdf (the proceedings PDF, 8 pages, read in full; text extracted with `pypdf`. The TUM repository copy <https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1287219/document.pdf> is the same paper.)  
**Inventory:** confirmed (the numbered items are Definitions 1-4, Propositions 1-7 and Algorithm 1, each with its own counter; there are no Theorems or Lemmas)

The origin of `ZonoBundle`: a bundle stores zonotopes and denotes their intersection, so
that intersection is exact and linear maps and Minkowski sums act on each member
separately. The thesis reproduces the idea in its §3.5.5 (see
[Althoff's thesis](../2010/althoff-thesis.md)); this is the paper where it first appeared,
with the propositions the thesis leaves unnumbered.

## Findings

**Eq. 2 — the halves are disjoint.** The paper says the split is exact, "i.e. `Z_∩¹ ∩ Z_∩² = ∅` and `Z_∩¹ ∪ Z_∩² = Z_∩`". The union is true, for any `Λ` and any box. The disjointness is not: the halves are closed and cut at a hyperplane, so both contain the cut. In one dimension `Z_∩ = {[-1, 1]}`, `Λ = 1` splits into `[-1, 0]` and `[0, 1]`, which share `0`. What the halves share is `Z_∩` cut with `Ψ` minus its `j`-th generator. [`SplitInter.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/ZonoBundle/Real/Theorems/SplitInter.lean), [`SplitNotDisjoint.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/ZonoBundle/Real/Theorems/SplitNotDisjoint.lean)

**Prop. 7 — the iff.** The paper prints `Z_∩ ∩ S ≠ ∅ ↔ ∀ i, Z_i ∩ S ≠ ∅` and gives "associativity of intersection" as the reason. That gives `Z_∩ ∩ S = ⋂ᵢ (Z_i ∩ S)`, and a nonempty `Z_i ∩ S` for each `i` does not make their intersection nonempty: `Z₁ = [0,1]`, `Z₂ = [2,3]`, `S = [0,3]`. Only `→` holds, and the text uses only its contrapositive. [`IntersectsOfIntersects.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/ZonoBundle/Real/Theorems/IntersectsOfIntersects.lean), [`NotIntersectsImp.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/ZonoBundle/Real/Theorems/NotIntersectsImp.lean)

**`Sec. VI-A` — what the guard intersection leaves open.** The section names the steps (halfspaces from parallelotope enclosures, intersection with the guard by a standard toolbox, parallelotope enclosures of the intersection's vertices) and none of their parameters: which directions the halfspaces take, how the vertices are enumerated, and which directions the final parallelotopes take. `ZonoBundle.guardIntersect` takes all three as arguments, the vertex enumerator as a function of the polytope it enumerates, and `guardIntersect_outer` proves the enclosure for any choice whose vertices cover the cut and whose directions are invertible.

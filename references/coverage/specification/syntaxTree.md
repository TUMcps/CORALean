# specification/@syntaxTree

CORA methods of `specification/@syntaxTree/` (a class of `specification/` or `specification/signals/`; a single-file class lists its public methods, constructor included), one row each, and where CORALean answers them. Conventions, states and the out-of-scope reasons are in [../README.md](../README.md).

**CORALean counterpart:** `SyntaxTree` (`src/CORALean/Specification/SyntaxTree/`), CORA's expression tree of the forward-backward contractor: a leaf variable or an operator node holding the interval that encloses its value over the box and the `funHan` that narrows its operand's interval. It is not related to `STL.Expr` (a predicate's expression, evaluated at a point); `eval` is the function a tree denotes and `Sound` says its intervals and `funHan`s are correct.

**CORALean counterpart:** `TrajectoryAnalyzer` (`src/CORALean/Specification/TrajectoryAnalyzer/`), the propositions `aps` (the `containers.Map` as a list of pairs in the order of `keys(aps)`, over `AtomicProposition`) and the simulation result `traj`, of which `TrajectoryAnalyzer.Trajectory` keeps only the fields `analyze` reads: the time points `t`, the points `x l s` of realization `s` at the time with index `l` (numbered from `1` as in MATLAB), in the scalar the simulation is stored in, and the locations `loc` of a hybrid automaton, one per time, as a list (empty without locations). `TrajectoryAnalyzer.SignalsRepresent` says what a list of Boolean signals expresses.

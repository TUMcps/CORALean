# specification/@onlineReachSetAnalyzer

CORA methods of `specification/@onlineReachSetAnalyzer/` (a class of `specification/` or `specification/signals/`; a single-file class lists its public methods, constructor included), one row each, and where CORALean answers them. Conventions, states and the out-of-scope reasons are in [../README.md](../README.md).

**CORALean counterpart:** `OnlineReachSetAnalyzer` (`src/CORALean/Specification/OnlineReachSetAnalyzer/`), the syntax DAG of an STL formula as a list of `OnlineAnalyzerNode`s (numbered from `1`, the root first) over the atomic propositions of `AtomicProposition`, with `leaves`, `numObservations` and `propagated` as in CORA; a method returns the new analyzer where CORA's handle object is mutated. The formula is taken after `desugar` as `OnlineReachSetAnalyzer.Formula` (`true`, `false`, atoms, `~`, `&`, `|`, `until`); an atom stands both for a `variable` that holds an `atomicProposition` and for a comparison after `convert2set`. `eps` (MATLAB's `eps`) is a parameter of the methods that propagate. Whether the verdict is right for the whole formula, in the four-valued semantics, is the theory of [Lercher and Althoff, CAV 2024](../../2024/lercher-cav-four-valued-stl.md), not proved here.

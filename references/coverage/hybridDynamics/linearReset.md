## Details

**`resolve` — the conditions.** At the state `x` and global input `u` it equals the original reset
at any stacked input `w` whose bound entries are those outputs and global entries those of `u`,
given the synchronized reset's block structure of `B` (component `i`'s columns hold only its rows),
`stateBinds` injective and `D(l,:)` pointing only at global inputs (CORA throws otherwise).

# contSet/@polygon

CORA methods of `contSet/@polygon/`, one row each, and where CORALean answers them. Conventions, states and the out-of-scope reasons are in [../README.md](../README.md).

**CORALean counterpart:** `Polygon`, a finite union of convex 2-D polytopes: `SetUnion (VPolytope (Vec ℝ 2))` (`Polygon.Real.Polygon`) and its float analogue (`Polygon.Float.Polygon`). A polygon is built from convex pieces; CORA's `polyshape` boundary lists (holes, regions) need a triangulation, which is not formalized, so the methods that read the boundary or are polygon boolean operations stay `—` unless a region-level statement captures them (R-PG in [../README.md](../README.md)).

# contSet/@polygon

CORA methods of `contSet/@polygon/`, one row each, and where CORALean answers them. Conventions, states and the out-of-scope reasons are in [../README.md](../README.md).

**CORALean counterpart:** `Polygon`, a finite union of convex 2-D polytopes: `SetUnion (VPolytope (Vec ℝ 2))` (`Polygon.Real.Polygon`) and its float analogue (`Polygon.Float.Polygon`). A polygon is built from convex pieces; CORA's `polyshape` boundary lists (holes, regions) need a triangulation of the boundary, which is not formalized (`Polygon.triangulation` is a cover of the pieces by vertex triangles), so the methods that read the boundary stay `—` unless a region-level statement captures them (R-PG in [../README.md](../README.md)); the boolean operations `subtract`, `mldivide` and `minkDiff` are stated for the regularized difference as a region, not as a polygon.

## Findings

**Thm. 1.** The centre shift reads `+ξ_E/2`; it is `−ξ_E/2` — under the orientation `E_{k₀,j} ≤ E_{k₀,i}`, which is what `Alg. 2`'s edges have and what `relaxExpEven_outer` takes as `hle`; with the roles reversed the sign is `+`, and `Thm. 1` as printed orders the two exponents nowhere. And the evenness of `E_{k₀,j}` is omitted, without which the shift is zero. CORA is unaffected by either. [`RelaxExp.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/PolyZonotope/Real/Operations/RelaxExp.lean)

**Alg. 2 — ties, the leaf and the update.** `greedy(G)` keeps "the outgoing edge with the smallest edge weight", which is not one edge where two outgoing edges weigh the same; `i ← leaf node` does not say which leaf; and "Update G (remove (i,j), update indices)" is bookkeeping on an indexed graph. The formalization takes the least target and the least leaf, and runs lines 1 and 2 again on the relaxed set: the merge drops the exponent column of the leaf `i`, which no kept edge enters, and keeps `j`'s, so the other nodes keep their lightest edges, except that a tie can then fall the other way, the merged generator moving to the last index. None of it touches `Thm. 2`: `relaxGreedy_outer` holds for any of these choices.

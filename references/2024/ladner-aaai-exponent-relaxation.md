## Findings

[FINDINGS.md](../../FINDINGS.md) — **Thm. 1**: the centre shift reads `+ξ_E/2`
where it is `−ξ_E/2`, and the evenness of `E_{k₀,j}` is omitted, without which
the shift is zero. CORA is unaffected by either.

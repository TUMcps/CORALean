# Computing Reachable Sets of Hybrid Systems Using a Combination of Zonotopes and Polytopes

M. Althoff, O. Stursberg and M. Buss. "Computing Reachable Sets of Hybrid Systems Using a Combination of Zonotopes and Polytopes". Nonlinear Analysis: Hybrid Systems, 4(2):233-249, 2010.

**Source:** https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1287515/file.pdf (the authors' manuscript in the TUM repository, "Preprint submitted to Elsevier 23 March 2009", 29 pages, read in full; text extracted with `pypdf`. The typeset journal version, doi:10.1016/j.nahs.2009.03.009, was not accessible; the numbering below is the manuscript's.)  
**Inventory:** confirmed (against the manuscript: Definitions 1-5, Lemma 6, Theorem 7 and Propositions 8-12 share one counter; Algorithm 1; equations (1)-(3))

The origin of CORA's `guardIntersect_polytope` and of the cross-product halfspace
conversion `zonotope/polytope` uses: reachable zonotopes are enclosed by polytopes where
they meet a guard, intersected there exactly, and enclosed again by a zonotope from the
vertices. Most of the paper is about the two conversions — a zonotope reduced to a
parallelotope or a smaller zonotope and then turned into halfspaces, and polytopes
enclosed by a zonotope along chosen directions.

## Findings

**`Alg. 1` — the loop condition.** The text says the reachable set within a location is
computed "until it has completely left the invariant ... or until the final time is
reached", which is a loop running while the set is nonempty *and* `t < t_f`. The listing
prints `while R^h([t_{k-1}, t_k]) ≠ ∅ or t < t_f`, which keeps iterating up to `t_f` after
the set has left the invariant and, if it never leaves, past `t_f`; and `t` is never
assigned in the listing. Read as the text says, `t = kr`.
[`ReachGuard.lean`](../../src/CORALean/HybridDynamics/Operations/ReachGuard.lean)

**`Sec. 5.4.2` — singular choices.** Method C ranks every choice of `n` generators by
`Θ̃ = |det[g^(i₁), ..., g^(iₙ)]|⁻¹`, which is undefined where the determinant vanishes, and
Prop. 8 then needs a full-rank `Γ`. The paper says nothing of either, nor of a zonotope with
fewer than `n` generators. `methodCBasis` drops the singular choices and falls back to
`Γ = I`, a box, when none is left, so `methodC_outer` holds unconditionally.
[`MethodCBasis.lean`](../../src/CORALean/ContSet/Zonotope/Real/Operations/MethodCBasis.lean)

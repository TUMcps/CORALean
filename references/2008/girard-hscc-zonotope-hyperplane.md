# Zonotope/Hyperplane Intersection for Hybrid Systems Reachability Analysis

A. Girard and C. Le Guernic. "Zonotope/Hyperplane Intersection for Hybrid Systems Reachability Analysis". In Hybrid Systems: Computation and Control (HSCC), LNCS 4981, Springer, 2008, pp. 215-228.

**Source:** https://www.cecs.uci.edu/~papers/cpsweek08/papers/hscc08/session1/s1-2.pdf (the authors' manuscript in the LNCS format from the HSCC 2008 proceedings, 14 pages, read in full; text extracted with `pypdf`; the Springer DOI is 10.1007/978-3-540-78929-1_16)  
**Inventory:** confirmed (the numbered items are Proposition 1, Algorithms 1-4, Remarks 1-2 and the display of equation (1); the unnumbered statements that make a claim are rows named by section)

The paper behind CORA's `guardIntersect_zonoGirard` (`location/guardIntersect`'s `'zonoGirard'` method): the intersection of a reachable-set zonotope with a guard hyperplane is over-approximated in finitely many directions `D` by projecting onto the plane spanned by the hyperplane's normal `n` and a direction `ℓ` (`Prop. 1`) and bounding the line `x = γ` against the plane image (`Alg. 2`), with the line routine `BOUND_INTERSECT_2D` of `Alg. 3` and `Alg. 4`. CORA takes `D` to be the directions of a basis (`Sec. 3.4`) and returns a zonotope bundle of the boxes. Formalized here: the intersection test, the projection identity, the dimension-reduction step with `BOUND_INTERSECT_2D` as an argument that owes the output specification of `Alg. 3` and `Alg. 4` (`IsBoundIntersect2D`), and that a polyhedron with orthonormal normals is a zonotope. The scanning of `Alg. 3` and the dichotomic search of `Alg. 4` are not formalized: they need the structure of a zonogon's boundary, which the tree does not have. The halfspace form of a zonotope (`Zonotope.hRep_eq`) gives the section of a zonogon by `x = γ` as an intersection of one interval per generator; what is missing is that the least and greatest ends lie on the edges of the two chains, with the generators sorted by angle (the facet with a given normal is the chain edge at that generator, the free parallel generators and the vertical edges on `L_γ` being its degenerate cases), and for `Alg. 4` that the bisection with the sum of the remaining generators as pivot finds those edges. CORA's method is formalized with `BOUND_INTERSECT_2D` replaced by the Lagrangian dual of the same linear program, a bound on the cut proved outer only ([`GuardIntersectZonoGirard.lean`](../../src/CORALean/HybridDynamics/Definitions/GuardIntersectZonoGirard.lean), [`GuardIntersectZonoGirardOuter.lean`](../../src/CORALean/HybridDynamics/Theorems/GuardIntersectZonoGirardOuter.lean)); neither algorithm is, so `Alg. 3` and `Alg. 4` stay `—`.

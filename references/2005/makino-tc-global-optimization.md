The range bounders behind CORA's `optLinQuad` and `globVerMinimization`: the linear dominated
bounder (LDB), which follows a model's linear part to bound its minimum and cut the domain
down, the quadratic fast bounder (QFB), which subtracts the positive semidefinite quadratic part,
and the box-list optimizer COSY-GO that uses them. All in the normalized coordinates of a model
over `[-1, 1]ⁿ`, with `Taylm.reexpand` for the re-expansion at a box's center.

# Float representations exempt from `scripts/check-absorb-rounding.py`: `<X>  <reason>` per line.
#
# Every float main type `ContSet/<X>/Float/<X>.lean` has an `I_error` box and an `absorbRounding`
# with `absorbRounding_outer` (CONTRIBUTING, "The error box and absorbRounding"). A representation
# that cannot hold the box, or has no box of its own, is listed here with the reason. The list
# only shrinks: an entry whose representation meets the rule is reported as stale.
Interval  is the error-box type itself: its operations round outward in the bounds
Polygon  an abbreviation for a union of float `VPolytope`s; the pieces carry the boxes
ProbZonotope  the rounding sits in the mean zonotope's `I_error` and in the covariance interval, which is outward by construction
VPolytope  absorbing a box needs the box's 2^n corners at each of the `k` vertices, a vertex count the representation cannot hold in general
ZonoBundle  no box of its own: each member zonotope carries one, and the set is their intersection
Zoo  a set of functions, with no ambient point set to hold a box; the members are a Taylor model and an interval
